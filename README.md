# Boo 👻 - macOS Dock Companion Desktop Pet

**Boo** is a native macOS background desktop pet built in Swift and SwiftUI. Boo is a cute, spooky procedural ghost that gently floats back and forth along the top line of the macOS Dock, bobbing up and down, looking around, performing acrobatic flips, thinking emoji thoughts, falling asleep when you are idle, and waking up with a happy jump when clicked.

---

## 🌟 Features & Behaviors

1. **Gentle Floating Glide**: Boo glides at a calm, serene floating speed (`0.8 px/frame`) across the Dock.
2. **👀 Mouse Cursor Staring & Head Tracking**: When your mouse pointer approaches Boo, he stops and turns his eyes and head to follow your cursor.
3. **✨ Cartwheel Flips**: Boo performs periodic 360° airborne cartwheels with glowing sparkle particles.
4. **💭 Emoji Thought Bubbles**: Floating speech bubbles appear above Boo's head displaying thought emojis (☕️, 🎃, 👻, 🍕, ⭐️, 🎮, 🎵, 💬, 🌙, 🍭).
5. **🌙 Nighttime & Dark Mode Glow**: In macOS Dark Mode or at night (8 PM – 6 AM), Boo glows with a soft spectral aura and floating Will-o'-the-Wisp flames.
6. **😮 Startled Reaction**: Sweeping your mouse rapidly past Boo startles him into a gasp `(O o O)` and sudden upward float!
7. **🔍 Look Around**: Boo pauses periodically, tilting his head and glancing left and right.
8. **💤 Sleep & Click-to-Wake**: After 3 minutes of inactivity, Boo rests on the Dock bar with closed eyes `(u u)` and floating `z Z Z` particles. Click Boo while sleeping to trigger a happy wake-up jump!
9. **Background Agent (`LSUIElement = true`)**: Runs cleanly as a menu bar app (`Quit Boo`).
